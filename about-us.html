About EasyFileScanner

A free, simple document scanner for your phone. No ads and no hidden charges.

EasyFileScanner was built for people who just want to turn paper into clean, shareable files without clutter: students, office workers, shop owners, freelancers and families. Open the app, tap Scan, and you're done.

What EasyFileScanner does

Smart scanning

Detects edges, crops and straightens each page. Scan several pages in one go.

One-tap filters

Original, Auto, Black & White or Grayscale for clear, readable scans.

PDF & JPG export

Save scans as PDF or JPG, ready to print, email or upload.

Organize & search

Rename scans, search by title, rotate and reorder pages, swipe to delete with undo.

Share instantly

Send scans via WhatsApp, email or any app on your phone.

Private by design

Your scans are stored only on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Our approach

Your scans belong to you. Documents are stored only on your phone, and nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it. We do not offer server backup, so your documents are never stored on our servers.

Our commitment

  • No ads. The app is free to use, and optional in-app purchases are only a way to support the developer.
  • We never sell your personal data or your scanned documents.
  • We collect only what's needed to run your account and keep the app working. Your scanned documents are never uploaded to us.
  • We're upfront about how the app works. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
