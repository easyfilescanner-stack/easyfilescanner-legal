Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 9 October 2026

EasyFileScanner ("we", "our", "the app") is a document scanning app developed by EasyFileScanner. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used and stored, and your rights.

1. Account information

You can sign in with Google or with email. Depending on the method, we collect:

  • Name and email address (from your Google account or entered by you)
  • Password if you use email sign-in (stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot see it)
  • Usage and performance data (such as app opens, screens viewed, features used, session length, app speed and performance, and approximate region derived from your network), collected through Firebase Analytics. This never includes the content of your scanned documents
  • Crash reports and device diagnostics (device model, operating system and app version, crash logs and an app installation identifier), collected through Firebase Crashlytics
  • Push notification token (a device identifier issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging), so the app can send you notifications

This is used only to create and secure your account, keep you signed in until you log out, identify which backup belongs to you, and deliver notifications. Usage, performance and crash data is used only to understand how the app is used, find and fix bugs, and improve speed and stability. We do not use any of this for advertising, profiling or marketing.

2. Your scanned documents

  • Stored on your device. Scans, thumbnails, titles and page information are saved locally on your phone.
  • Nothing is uploaded automatically. Your documents leave your device only when you tap Backup, or when you share or export a file yourself.
  • Backup. If you choose to back up, your scans and related app data are uploaded to our servers and linked to your account so you can restore them on a new phone.

We do not read, analyze, or use the content of your documents for any purpose other than storing and returning your backup to you.

4. Third-party services

The app uses services that process data on our behalf or to provide features:

  • Google Sign-In, for authentication (Google's Privacy Policy applies to that step).
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner, which detects and crops pages. Scanning is performed by Google's on-device component.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google), used to measure app usage and performance so we can improve the app. Analytics data is not used for advertising and is not sold. See Google's Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google), used to collect crash reports and diagnostics so we can fix bugs. See Google's Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), used to send push notifications. It processes a device token and basic technical information to deliver messages (see Google's Firebase privacy information).
  • Google Play Billing, which processes optional in-app purchases (voluntary support/donations). Payments are handled entirely by Google; we do not receive or store your card or payment details, only confirmation of whether a purchase is active.
  • Our own backend server, hosted on a third-party free-tier hosting plan, which stores your account details and backups. The hosting provider does not use your data for its own purposes, sell it, or share it with advertisers.

The app contains no advertising and no ad networks.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) and password hashing.

Honest disclosure: No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Our server runs on free-tier hosting, which does not guarantee uptime or automatic backups. We recommend keeping your own copies of important documents.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only:

  • With infrastructure providers strictly to host and run the app (Section 4);
  • When you explicitly share or export a document yourself;
  • If required by law.

7. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear app data. Backup data is kept on our servers until you delete your account or request deletion.

8. Your rights & account deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated backup data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete My Account
  • Online: see our Delete Account page, or email easyfilescanner@gmail.com

Deleting your account permanently removes your backed-up data from our servers. Files stored only on your device are removed when you delete them or uninstall the app.

9. Children's privacy

EasyFileScanner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
