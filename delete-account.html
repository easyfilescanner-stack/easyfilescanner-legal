Delete Your Account

How to delete your EasyFileScanner account and backup data.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open EasyFileScanner and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete My Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Option 2: By email

Send an email to YOUR_EMAIL@example.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my EasyFileScanner account". We will process your request within a reasonable time.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details (name, email, sign-in information)
  • All backups stored on our servers
Scans stored only on your phone are not affected. Delete them in the app or uninstall the app to remove them. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
